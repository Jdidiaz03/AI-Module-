Operating model

What the copilot does after a case is created.

The app is designed as an enterprise decision workbench. It does not just summarize files; it builds a case record that pricing, operations, finance, sales leadership, and the BD can inspect.

01

Capture the intake and create a case record

Required fields, files, and optional context are stored with a case ID, opportunity type, status, files, and an audit-ready status trail.

Uploaded
02

Extract evidence and mark assumptions

The system captures source file, source excerpt, confidence, review status, and missing data. Unsourced facts are labeled as assumptions, not treated as normal evidence.

Parsed
03

Run guardrails and pricing scenarios

Serviceability rules and aggressive, balanced, and conservative pricing scenarios show what can continue, what needs review, and what requires approval.

Checked
04

Draft, verify, approve, then export

The recommendation includes serviceable scope, out-of-scope scope, commercial strategy, risk, follow-up questions, and client-facing message. Export stays disabled until verifier and human approval pass.
