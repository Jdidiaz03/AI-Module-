The business problem

Enterprise opportunity reviews take too long because the evidence is scattered.

Business developers need to reconcile RFQs, service capabilities, pricing rules, cost guardrails, CRM notes, customer emails, and historical outcomes before leadership can decide how to proceed. The copilot turns that material into a structured recommendation.

Controlled decision pipeline

From opportunity pack to approved recommendation.

01

Upload the opportunity pack

Load RFQs, CRM notes, email threads, service slides, pricing workbooks, and historical opportunity data.

02

Extract the facts

Convert unstructured and structured sources into client profile, volume, geography, requirements, and pain points.

03

Check guardrails

Apply service rules and financial thresholds before the AI drafts any commercial recommendation.

04

Approve the output

Review evidence, assumptions, risks, pricing scenarios, and client-facing export before submission.
