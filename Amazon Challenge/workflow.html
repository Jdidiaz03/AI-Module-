Project overview flow

Intake, extraction, guardrails, pricing, recommendation, evidence, export.

Those seven tabs are the backbone of the v1 case workflow. The important part is that the system does not jump from upload to proposal; it creates reviewable evidence at every step.

01 Intake

Create the case from the opportunity pack

The BD uploads files, company details, opportunity type, monthly volume, region, and commercial context. The case starts with status Uploaded.

Uploaded

02 Extraction

Turn source files into structured facts

Geography, volume, weight, dimensions, SLA, returns, PUDO, peak demand, pricing inputs, strategic notes, and missing data are separated into an evidence table.

Extracted

03 Guardrails

Run deterministic serviceability checks

Rules return Pass, Watch, Needs review, or Blocked so AI output cannot hide geography, SLA, parcel profile, returns, PUDO, dangerous goods, or missing-data issues.

Checked

04 Pricing

Compare aggressive, balanced, and conservative scenarios

Each scenario shows serviceable volume, estimated cost, proposed price, contribution margin, approval requirement, and notes.

Calculated

05 Approval

Verify claims before export

The final recommendation stays locked until citations, assumptions, forbidden claims, pricing review, and the human approval checklist are complete.

Export locked

Give reviewers a recommendation they can inspect.

Start with a source-backed case file, then let pricing, operations, finance, and sales leadership review the same evidence before export.
