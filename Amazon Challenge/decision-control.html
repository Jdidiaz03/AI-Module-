Why it matters

Keep serviceability, pricing, and evidence rules in front of the reviewer.

The project plan is explicit: deterministic guardrails must be visible, auditable, and impossible for AI output to hide. This page explains the decision controls that keep the recommendation honest.

  • Rules decide what can be proposed

    Spain Peninsula and Balearics can continue through review; returns, PUDO, dangerous goods, and unsupported geography cannot be folded into a clean recommendation.

  • Margins decide who must approve

    Pricing scenarios show contribution margin and approval requirement, so below-threshold deals are not presented as simple approvals.

  • Evidence decides what can be exported

    Every important claim has to connect to a source fact, a guardrail rule, or an assumption label before it appears in client-facing output.

A case file, not a chatbot answer.

Guardrail table

The checks the reviewer should see before pricing.

Rule Project value Review result
Serviceable geography Spain Peninsula and Balearics are in scope; unsupported regions are separated. Pass or watch
Delivery promise 1-2 day delivery expectation must be checked against scope. Needs review
Parcel profile 15kg max weight and 80x80x60 cm max dimensions. Pass or blocked
Premium services OTP EUR 0.35, SOD EUR 0.10, Balearics 1.35x multiplier. Pricing input
Out-of-scope services Returns, PUDO, and dangerous goods are not included in a clean proposal. Blocked

Pricing thresholds

Margin status changes the decision label.

Target

At or above 21% contribution margin can support a stronger pursue recommendation.

Minimum acceptable

At or above 13% can continue, but the recommendation should explain the tradeoff.

VP approval

Below 13% requires escalation; below 9% becomes an automatic no-go threshold.
